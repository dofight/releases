# Dofight

[Français](README.md) · **English**

The fight assistant for Dofus: a panel beside the game that shows, for the monsters of your zone or your fight,
what can cost you the fight (one-shots, invulnerabilities, mechanics), their resistances and their spells.

## Install

1. Go to the latest release: [Releases](https://github.com/dofight/releases/releases/latest).
2. Download `Dofight_..._x64-setup.exe` and run it.
3. In Dofus, turn on **“Show map coordinates”** in the options: Dofight reads the zone name just above them.
4. For Dofight to open on its own in fights, keep the icon bar in the top-left corner of the game expanded.

Dofight then updates itself.

## What it does

- The monsters of your zone or dungeon, with badges (danger, tip, mechanic) and a summary drawn from the guides.
- **Songe mode**: hover a monster group on the map and Dofight shows it.
- Search for a dungeon, a zone or a monster.
- Opens and closes on its own when a fight starts and ends (option).
- Themes matching the Dofus interface; interface in French or English.

Dofight only looks at the screen: it sends nothing to the game, reads neither its memory nor its network traffic,
and never takes the focus.

Data: [Dofensive](https://dofensive.com), [DofusDB](https://dofusdb.fr).
